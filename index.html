The quiet problem

Great products lose to clearer ones.

Your homepage lists features. Buyers want to see the outcome.

They give it a few seconds, can’t picture the product in their day, and leave for the competitor who showed them.

You explain the same thing on every call.

The founder is the best salesperson in the company, and also the most expensive one. They can’t be on every call.

Your posts get a scroll, not a stop.

Text and stock graphics blend into the feed. A story that moves is the thing people stop for and send to a colleague.
